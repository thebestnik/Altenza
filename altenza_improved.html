Visa Classifications

Explore Albania Visa Categories.

Albania offers specific entry routes depending on your nationality, planned activity, and length of stay. Select a visa category below to review requirements.

Long-Stay • Type D

Business & Investor Visa

For entrepreneurs, company founders, and foreign investors registering a commercial entity (QKB) or investing capital in Albania.

  • Stay Duration: 1 year initial residence, renewable up to 5 years
  • Key Documents: QKB company extract, corporate charter, bank guarantee
  • Permit Type: Single Permit combining work and residence authorization
Processing: 4–6 Weeks Inquire about Business Visa
Employment • Type D

Executive & Work Visa

For executives, managing directors, and specialized professionals contracted by an employer registered in the Republic of Albania.

  • Stay Duration: Duration of employment contract (typically 1 to 2 years)
  • Key Documents: Employment contract, apostilled degree, criminal record
  • Article 74: Fast-track executive single permit with employer sponsorship
Processing: 4–5 Weeks Inquire about Work Permit
Remote Work • Single Permit

Digital Nomad Permit

For remote employees, freelancers, and independent contractors working for clients outside Albania with qualifying foreign income.

  • Stay Duration: 1 year initial permit, renewable for up to 2 additional years
  • Income Criteria: Documented foreign contracts or remote employer verification
  • Lifestyle: Live along the Adriatic/Ionian Riviera with low living costs
Processing: 3–5 Weeks Check Nomad Eligibility
Short-Stay • Type C

Short-Stay & Tourism Visa

For leisure holidays, business scouting trips, commercial meetings, and family visits for stays up to 90 days within any 180-day period.

  • Stay Duration: Up to 90 days (Single or Multiple Entry available)
  • Visa Exemptions: US, EU, UK, and Schengen visa holders often enter visa-free
  • Key Documents: Return flight booking, proof of accommodation & travel insurance
Processing: 10–15 Days Verify Visa-Free Status
